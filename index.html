Our Heritage & Passion

Redefining Fine Dining in Faisalabad

Shahryar Restaurant was founded on a simple yet unyielding commitment: to elevate classic Pakistani and international flavors through meticulous craftsmanship and uncompromised hospitality.

Nestled in the bustling heart of Akbarabad on Jail Road, our establishment merges the warm nostalgia of traditional live-fire cooking with a refined, contemporary atmosphere. Every dish is a testament to our relentless search for pristine ingredients, hand-ground aromatic spices, and time-honored cooking techniques.

2011

The Beginning

Started as a humble family BBQ house dedicated to authentic clay-oven cooking.

2018

Culinary Expansion

Expanded menu to include Desi Karahi, Chinese fusion, and premium executive platters.

2026

Luxury Flagship Experience

Transformed into a state-of-the-art fine dining sanctuary for families and connoisseurs.
